Side Panel Chat for Claude

Unofficial extension. Not made by or affiliated with Anthropic.

Create one at console.anthropic.com. The key is stored only in this browser profile and is sent only to api.anthropic.com. Usage is billed to your Anthropic account.

Higher effort lets Claude think longer. It helps with hard questions but is slower and costs more. Medium suits most chats.

How to use it

  • Click the extension's toolbar button, or press Alt+Shift+C, to open the side panel.
  • Click the paperclip to attach the page you're viewing, including any text you've selected.
  • Right-click selected text and choose Ask Claude about…, or right-click a page and choose Summarize this page with Claude.

Privacy

Pages are only read when you attach one or use the right-click menu, and are then sent to the Anthropic API with your message. Nothing is sent anywhere else, and conversations aren't saved: closing the side panel or starting a new chat clears them.
